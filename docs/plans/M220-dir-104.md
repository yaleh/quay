  `doc-*` gates are registered by `registerDocumentGate()` (L71–73) from `DOCUMENT_GATE_IDS`

## Rollback

- **Mid-stage:** a failed Stage-1/2/3 command is fixed in-place within the same worktree before
  proceeding (no partial Land); the RED evidence for Stage 1 is preserved before Stage 2 flips it.
- **Post-Land regression:** the full-suite GREEN is a Land precondition; a regression surfacing
  only on `master` after Land (e.g. `gate-config-loader.test.mjs` or `gate.test.mjs` breaking — a
  `loadWorkspaceGates`/`readGatesConfig` contract violation, G3) triggers
  `quay retreat DIR-104 --reason <specific>`, a fix commit, and a fresh acceptance-gate run — the
  `tasks/DIR-104.md` status ticks written at Land are un-ticked (DIR-020 discipline) until the
  re-run passes.
- **Provenance drift:** if the CST rewrite changed the parse shape for a workspace whose
  existing gates resolve differently than before (it must NOT — branch structure and fail-quiet
  shapes are preserved, G1/G3), that is a REFUTED-finding candidate for the acceptance audit
  (deviation-log per DIR-017), not a silent fixture tweak.

## Plan-check stopping rule

The PlanAuthor/PlanCheck loop follows the standardized rule: at most **3** Plan-check rounds;
success only when `F_i = 0` (zero findings) on a round. After round 3 with `F_i > 0`, the Plan is
returned for revision rather than dispatched — never a silent proceed.
