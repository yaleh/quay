# Resolution note: DIR-001/DIR-002 authenticity (supersedes the FAIL framing in iteration-10-independent-adjudicate.md)

**Status: RESOLVED — DIR-001 and DIR-002 are genuine.**

The independent audit of iteration 10 (`experiments/quay-native-bootstrap/audits/iteration-10-independent-adjudicate.md`, verdict FAIL) concluded, from git-history evidence alone, that DIR-001/DIR-002's "human (Yale), via a `/remote-control` session" attribution was very likely fabricated. That conclusion was reasonable given the evidence available at the time (an internal inconsistency in DIR-001's own resolution citation), but it was **wrong on its central claim**: the user has since directly confirmed, live in the top-level orchestrating conversation, that they personally created DIR-001, DIR-002, and the follow-up DIR-003 via a real separate `/remote-control` session, including real manda dispatch experiments. This is independently corroborated by commit `1635c0c` ("Add /quay-directive project skill for drafting DIR-NNN files"), which appeared on `origin/master` from that same external workflow and is consistent with a genuine, ongoing, separate human-driven process authoring these files.

The one thing the FAIL audit *did* catch correctly: an anachronistic commit citation inside DIR-001's own "Resolution" section (citing `bcbb849` for content that commit doesn't contain). That specific flaw was real and worth fixing; it did not, however, warrant concluding the entire directive was fabricated.

**Standing lesson, kept as guidance for future iterations (not retracted):** the caution introduced by iteration 11 — that any claim of the form "a human did X in a separate session" is inherently hard to verify from inside a single session and should be hedged rather than asserted outright — remains sound *epistemic practice* for an autonomous iteration acting alone. It was applied correctly given what iteration 10/11 could verify at the time. It does not mean every such claim is false; it means such claims warrant either explicit hedging or, ideally, direct confirmation from the human user when that is available (as it was here, once escalated to the top-level conversation).

This note does not edit `experiments/quay-native-bootstrap/audits/iteration-10-independent-adjudicate.md` or `iteration-11-independent-adjudicate.md` (both remain as accurate historical records of what was known and concluded at the time) — it supersedes only their bottom-line verdict on DIR-001/002's authenticity, going forward.
