## VT trajectory & slope — seeding the threshold from live data (§6.1 line 269)

Raw chart-relative sequence: `[0.8225 (m0), 0.8825 (m1), 0.8825 (m2), 0.8444 (m3, chart transition —
not directly comparable to m2 in raw terms), 0.7894 (m4, re-score correction), 0.7894 (m5, unchanged)]`
(all normalized to fraction-of-current-chart-max for cross-chart comparability).

**Finding: a naive point-to-point slope over these 5 milestones is NOT a meaningful exit signal yet**,
because:
- 2 of 5 milestones (m2, m5) are methodology-infrastructure work, explicitly zero-VT BY DESIGN
  (their value is measured on discovery-latency/ρ/φ tracks, not VT) — including them in a raw slope
  mechanically drags it toward zero regardless of real progress.
- 1 milestone (m3) is a chart transition — its "Δv" is a new surface's first-ever score, not
  incremental growth on an existing surface; not comparable to a same-chart Δv.
- 1 milestone (m4) is a re-score/correction pass — its negative Δv reflects a MEASUREMENT catching
  up to always-true product state (MD-001 merge-drift existed before m4, m4 only discovered and
  measured it), not new degradation introduced by m4's own work.
- Only ONE milestone (m1) is a "clean" same-chart capability-growth Δv with no confound. 1 data point
  is not a trend.

**Threshold definition seeded now, for use starting at checkpoint 2 (m10):** compute slope only over
milestones whose Δv is a direct, same-chart, non-corrective capability score (excluding by-design-
zero-VT methodology milestones and chart-transition/re-score milestones from the numerator, but
still counting them in the milestone-count denominator so they can't be used to dilute an
artificially-inflated apparent rate). Soft alarm at slope < +1.0 points/milestone on that basis,
computed over a trailing window of the last 5 SUCH qualifying milestones once ≥3 exist. Not enough
qualifying milestones exist yet (only m1) to compute or apply this at cp-01 — recorded here so cp-02
has a live, pre-declared definition rather than one invented after the fact.
