## Finding

**WHAT:** Fresh M103 archguard measurement (scope 77856690, noCache:true) shows `serve.ts.startServer` outDegree=7, not 6. The M100 AC (ARCH-M93-003) documented outDegree=6→6 (no regression). The discrepancy is the `serve-handlers.ts.handleAllRoutes` dependency, added by the M100 decomposition. `startServer` now calls `handleAllRoutes` (the extracted route dispatcher) — this is a new structural edge not present at M93 baseline and not counted in the M100 AC measurement.

**WHERE:** `packages/quay/src/serve.ts`, function `startServer` (file: 101 lines post-M100 decomposition).

**METRIC (M103 fresh measurement, scope 77856690, noCache:true, 2026-07-22):**
- outDegree = 7 (vs M100 AC claim of 6; vs M93 baseline of 6)

**WHY:** The M100 AC stated "outDegree=6 (same as M93 baseline, no regression)". The M100 decomposition extracted 16 handler functions into `serve-handlers.ts`, which reduced body size from 675L→65L. The M100 measurement likely used a scope where this edge was not captured, or the `handleAllRoutes` dependency was added post-measurement. Either way, the M103 fresh measurement is authoritative: outDegree=7.

**NOTE:** outDegree=7 still exceeds the original ≤4 threshold from ARCH-M93-002 intent, but since ARCH-M93-003 DoD was closed with "outDegree metric updated to: outDegree=6 (no regression)", this finding is a post-close regression at the metric level (+1).
