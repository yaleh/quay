Source: `experiments/quay-perpetual-stream/directives/archive/DIR-013-reconcile-auto-merged-task-to-plan-proposal-and-stale-plan-cross-references.md`

A human-directed `proposal-to-plan` run and the autonomous exp5 milestone M17-task-to-plan-skill-design independently designed the *same* new skill (`quay-task-to-plan`) at the same time, both writing `docs/proposals/exp5-quay-task-proposal-plan-skill.md`. Their commits interleaved on `master` and the proposal-file conflict was auto-resolved at merge `989e0cd` (taking M17 iteration-0's body wholesale). A read-only cross-document audit found three MUST-FIX defects, all confined to the two design docs: (F1) dangling internal cross-references in §§12-19 to non-existent §8.4/§8.5/§8-point-6/§11; (F2) status header + TOC say "§12-18" but the file carries a §19 (self-contradictory); (F3) the companion plan `docs/plans/3-7-quay-task-to-plan-skill.md` cites proposal subsections that never existed and never references the Part II (§12-19) that is the real implementer-facing spec. Nothing breaks now (M18 builds only against the untouched §4/§5/§6), but the record is self-contradictory and the plan is stale enough to misdirect the next consumer (the skill-implementation milestone). Grounding correction "no `task write` subcommand; `task edit` is the full-field path" verified as survived-clean in both docs.
