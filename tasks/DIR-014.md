Tracing DIR-012's execution through m17/m18/m19: the proposal→plan design had its scaffolding built (M18: sizing ceiling + a plan-time line-budget gate + a *written* two-class diversity policy) but not its engine, and no queued work will close the gap. DIR-012 item 3 (build the skill + dogfood) is not queued in backlog.md at all; M19 is documentation-only and its charter explicitly excludes building the skill or touching the M18-added sections; the `quay-task-to-plan` skill does not exist. Even if built, three structural facts mean subsequent development milestones still would NOT go through proposal→plan: (1) the policy is discretionary ("MAY, at a future charter's discretion") and preconditioned on a skill that doesn't exist; (2) OUTER-LOOP DISPATCH is unchanged — it never invokes the proposal→plan pipeline, which lives in inherited-core.md as prose only; (3) the one enforced gate (line-budget) only bites at >2000 declared lines and is bypassed by keeping milestones small — every milestone to date, including code-touching M16/M18, was chartered the old way, so the process is not even self-applied. This is the DIR-002 "enforcement half never built" pattern recurring on the proposal→plan mechanism itself. Requested: charter a development-class milestone that builds the skill, WIRES DISPATCH to invoke the pipeline, makes it the DEFAULT for capability-growth milestones (not discretionary; consider requiring a plan for any capability-growth charter, not only >2000-line ones), and dogfoods itself.
