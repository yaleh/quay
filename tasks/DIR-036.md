## Resolution

**dirStatus: rejected** (2026-07-23; supersedes the 2026-07-21 `deferred` note below).

REJECTED per the tightened `human-steered` semantics ([[DIR-062]], clause 2 — mission redirection).
DIR-036's own stated intent is to point the loop "**at a FOREIGN repo (archguard/meta-cc) as the
successor value source to exp5's saturated inward loop**" — i.e. to make a foreign product the loop's
objective. Under the human's 2026-07-23 decision, the loop's value source **stays quay itself**; the
loop MAY use foreign workspaces as validation/experimentation instruments (registry-gated, DIR-062
clause 3), but it does NOT adopt one as its mission. So this directive's requested action is void.

**The children's concrete value is retained** (rejection is of the parent's value-source-migration
intent, not of the real landings underneath it):
- [[DIR-036-A]] (status: done) — quay-the-product deployed on the real archguard workspace and drove a
  real task via the ABI. That real landing stands; it is exactly the kind of instrument-use DIR-062
  clause 3 now permits autonomously.
- [[DIR-036-B]] — the bounded V_meta transfer-test validation, now **superseded by [[DIR-061]]** (its
  archguard e2e proof) and autonomous-eligible under the drivable-workspaces registry once DIR-062's
  gate lands. Closed as superseded (see its own Resolution).

parent-done-iff-children holds: DIR-036-A done, DIR-036-B done (superseded). Terminal.
