## Proposal
Add a continuous EXTERNAL-dogfooding / discovery channel to the loop: a standing loop-driver routine that exercises quay from outside the experiment layer on a recurring cadence, filing evidence-backed findings as directive tasks.

## Finding
All product DIRs (DIR-037/039/040/041/042) came from manual human dogfooding/auditing, not from the autonomous loop's discovery engine. The loop has no standing external-dogfooding channel — it only discovers gaps when a human manually exercises the product.

## Requested action
Create a standing external-dogfooding routine in the loop-driver that exercises quay against a real foreign workspace (archguard) on a per-N-iterations cadence, filing real gaps as directive tasks.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Routine fires on a configurable cadence (every N iterations or on checkpoint).
- [ ] Routine exercises real product API against a foreign workspace.
- [ ] Findings filed as `label:directive` tasks with real evidence.

## Definition of Done
- [ ] At least one real dogfooding routine fire produces at least one real directive task.
- [ ] Routine is configurable via `.quay/loop.yml` (DIR-051 routine track).
