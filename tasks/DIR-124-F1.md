## Proposal

Split from DIR-124-F (M232) — mechanism 0a, the **template hygiene gate**. This child owns the
mechanical preflight fix that makes the recurring MECHANICAL preflight failures impossible:
`task-schema.ts` (both mirrors) rejects any non-`##`-heading content after `## Touches` at the real
parse site, so prose after Touches is never parsed as Touches globs (`touches-overbroad`), and the
rejection is surfaced through `prepare-admission-check.ts`'s preflight detector shape.

### Chosen mechanism

Per the parent's item 0a + Requested-action item 0:

1. **`task-schema.ts` parse-site change** — at `checkTouches`/`extractSection` (lines 368/411), reject
   any content after `## Touches` that is not a `##`-heading (i.e., prose/`- Files:` lines appended
   after `## Touches` are treated as Touches entries and trip `touches-overbroad`). Content appended
   AFTER `## Touches` is currently parsed as Touches entries (repo-ground-truth §3 third bullet); this
   gate rejects it so a template-format mistake fails closed at preflight instead of silently widening
   the Touches glob.
2. **`prepare-admission-check.ts` preflight detector shape** — surface the rejection through the
   existing detector shape `{code, blocking, disposition, policyVersion}` so the preflight report is
   typed and disposition-tracked (not a bare failure).
3. **M205 preservation** — `_stripWrappingBacktick`'s one-annotation strip stays intact: it already
   strips backticks AND one trailing ` (…)` annotation, so `loader.ts (new)` Touches entries still
   match cleanly. This gate must NOT regress that behavior.
4. Both `task-schema.ts` + `prepare-admission-check.ts` exist as byte-identical mirrors
   (`experiments/` + `plugin/`).

This removes the ~15% of batch cost from template-format mistakes before the registry matters.

**WIRING-CLAIM (F1-HYGIENE-GATE):** `prepare-milestone.js` preflight, via `prepare-admission-check.ts`,
rejects a task body whose post-`## Touches` content is non-`##`-heading prose (touches-overbroad),
while a `loader.ts (new)` Touches entry still matches cleanly (M205 preserved). → AC: template-hygiene
detector RED test (reject non-`##`-heading content after `## Touches`).
