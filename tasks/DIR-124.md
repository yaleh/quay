## Proposal

Implement the convergence sequence in
`docs/proposals/quay-milestone-workflow-git-crystallization.md` after [[DIR-117]] and [[DIR-119]]
complete, but before [[DIR-118]]. Turn the live milestone workflow from a prompt-coupled monolith
into a small executable control-plane kernel with one physical isolation service, hash-bound stage
receipts, narrow adapters, versioned execution policy, and resource-aware stage scheduling.

Split the work into ordered children:

1. [[DIR-124-A]] records stage observability, intentional invariants, and golden replay baselines.
2. Existing [[DIR-123]] restores real worktree isolation; the existing
   `gap-build-phase-iteration-evidence-path-not-single-sourced` then removes the remaining evidence
   root ambiguity.
3. [[DIR-124-B]] establishes canonical execution identity, journal, receipts, cache, and resume.
4. [[DIR-124-C]] extracts the deterministic kernel and Stage Adapter ABI.
5. [[DIR-124-D]] moves routing/gate/test/resource policy into a registry and deletes duplicate
   prompt/driver representations.
6. [[DIR-124-E]] uses the resulting boundaries for resource-aware cross-stage pipelining.
7. **[[DIR-124-F]] (registered 2026-08-01, human decision)** is the runtime-contract
   GroundTruthRegistry child (ADR-020).
