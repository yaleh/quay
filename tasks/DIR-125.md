## Proposal

Change `prepare-milestone` from an externally-restarted sequence of complete Proposal generations
into one bounded convergence run:

1. Run the independent Proposal authors and adjudicator exactly once for a preparation generation.
2. Run one full grounded Proposal review that returns typed findings with stable IDs, severity,
   blocking disposition, evidence, affected subsystem, and the claimed mechanism/AC relationship.
3. If blocking findings remain, dispatch one focused reviser against the current Proposal/task,
   preserving unrelated sections and resolving only the recorded findings; then perform a delta
   review against the previous Proposal hash and the unresolved finding ledger. Do not dispatch the
   original authors or adjudicator again.
4. Permit at most two delta-review rounds for an ordinary task (three for explicit `highRisk`), with
   a default soft wall budget of 45 minutes (75 minutes for `highRisk`). Check the budget before
   admitting another agent phase; never kill an already-running phase merely because the budget
   expires.
5. Only unresolved blocking findings prevent PlanAuthor. Valuable non-blocking findings must remain
   durable with one explicit disposition: `plan`, `split`, `accepted-risk`, `backlog`, `duplicate`,
   or `superseded`. They are not discarded, but they do not cause a new full Proposal synthesis.
6. Return `needs-human` with the ledger when the review cap/budget is exhausted or a split is
   recommended. Never auto-create a child task or silently waive a blocker.

The stopping boundary is not “the reviewer can think of nothing else.” Preparation succeeds when
the implementation no longer requires redesign, every safety-critical production behavior change
has a falsifiable AC or explicit accepted-risk decision, the touch set is conservative, and no
factual contradiction remains. Named regression tests, operator-facing pointer cleanup, and
adjacent risks may remain valuable Plan/backlog findings without recursively expanding Proposal
scope.

Keep the task Proposal/AC and checked `docs/plans/*.md` as content sources of truth. Store the
finding ledger as derived preparation metadata next to the existing preparation receipt; do not
copy the Proposal or Plan into it. Record timing and convergence counters so throughput can be
measured by candidate rather than inferred from session prose.

## Finding

DIR-120/M192 exposed a real unbounded-loop defect in DIR-117's newly landed preparation lifecycle.
The first ten real `prepare-milestone` attempts produced ProposalReview finding counts:

```text
8 → 2 → 1 → 1 → 1 → 2 → 2 → 1 → 2 → 1
```

Most findings were useful, including the DIR-026 split of Phase 3b into DIR-120-B, missing
production-behavior tests, a stale acceptance command pointing to M186 instead of M192, physical
deletion evidence, touch-set gaps, and stale operator pointers. The defect is not review quality.
It is orchestration: `prepare-milestone.js` gives PlanCheck a three-round revise/check loop, but a
nonzero ProposalReview immediately returns `revision-needed`. The caller then starts the entire
workflow again, so two new Proposal authors and a new adjudicator rewrite the complete Proposal
before every review. Each attempt therefore reviews a new object and can introduce new mechanism
claims; its finding count is not a monotone `F_i` sequence.

Measured through round 10, this consumed about 3h15m of active workflow time / 3h46m wall time,
roughly 1.13M output tokens, and about 6,191 cumulative changed task/charter lines for an 826-line
current result, without reaching PlanAuthor, PlanCheck, Receipt, Build, or Land. By comparison,
M185–M189 averaged 58.8 minutes per workflow attempt and 70.5 work-normalized minutes per delivered
task. A preparation stage whose retries are unbounded can erase the leaf-throughput gain already
demonstrated by the seven-task composite canary.

The existing 15–25 minute preparation planning range remains reasonable for ordinary candidates,
but only if full synthesis occurs once and later work is incremental. High-risk candidates may
spend more, but exceeding a declared budget must produce a split/human decision rather than an
eleventh complete regeneration.

## Requested action

1. Add an internal Proposal convergence loop to both `prepare-milestone.js` mirrors. Proposal
   authors and adjudicator run once per generation; subsequent rounds use a focused reviser and
   independent delta reviewer.
2. Replace the scalar `{findings, findingsDetail}` review result with a backward-compatible typed
   finding ledger. Each finding has a stable ID/fingerprint, severity, blocking boolean,
   disposition, subsystem, evidence, claim/AC mapping, and status.
3. Default to one full review plus at most two delta reviews; explicit `highRisk` permits one extra
   delta review. Add configurable arguments only within these fail-closed maxima—callers may lower
   the limits, not silently make them unbounded.
4. Add 45m/75m soft admission budgets and elapsed-time metrics. Budget expiry is checked before
   starting the next phase and returns `needs-human`; it does not terminate an in-flight agent.
5. Define blocking findings narrowly: factual contradiction, unresolved safety/fail-closed
   behavior, missing production callsite/ownership enforcement, a new behavior with no falsifiable
   AC or accepted-risk decision, stale acceptance wiring, or a scope cluster requiring split.
6. Preserve other valuable findings through explicit `plan`/`split`/`accepted-risk`/`backlog`/
   `duplicate`/`superseded` disposition. Only `blocking:true` findings prevent PlanAuthor.
7. Add a split checkpoint: recommend (but do not automatically perform) a split when one subsystem
   accumulates at least three independent blocking findings, when the candidate contains more than
   two independently landable mechanisms, or when the checked production touch set exceeds the
   configured small-milestone boundary.
8. Write the derived finding ledger and convergence metrics beside `preparation.json`; bind their
   hashes into the preparation receipt so a stale ledger cannot be paired with a newer Proposal.
9. Update OUTER-LOOP/quay-task-to-plan executable guidance to name the same stopping rule and
   budgets; remove any instruction that implies callers should restart full synthesis after every
   nonzero review.
10. Instrument at least: full-synthesis count, full/delta review counts, findings by severity and
    disposition, elapsed time by preparation phase, Proposal hashes, cumulative churn, terminal
    reason, and whether PlanAuthor was reached.
